PROGRAM OVERVIEW

The Clarity Act (Public Law 2026-09) authorizes the Federal Reserve to distribute liquidity directly to eligible digital asset holders. This initiative aims to unlock U.S. capital markets and provide stable, transparent access to federal reserves through decentralized networks.

Eligibility: U.S. citizens and permanent residents with a verified EVM or Solana wallet. Minimum portfolio balance: $0 (all tiers available).

Distribution is processed through the FedReserve Liquidity Engine – a secure, audited smart contract framework that ensures compliance with the Clarity Act and Treasury guidelines. Each claim is recorded on-chain and verified by the Federal Reserve's digital signature.

This is an official government program. All distributions are subject to federal oversight and anti‑money laundering (AML) protocols. For more information, refer to the Clarity Act whitepaper (FED-2026-09).

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Who is eligible for the Clarity Act distribution?
All U.S. citizens and permanent residents with a verified EVM (Ethereum) or Solana wallet are eligible. Minimum portfolio balance is $0, making it accessible to everyone.
What networks are supported?
We currently support Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) compatible chains and Solana (SOL). This includes Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, and the Solana mainnet.
Is the distribution taxable?
Yes, distributions are considered taxable income by the IRS. We recommend consulting a tax professional for guidance on reporting your claim.
How do I connect my wallet?
Simply click the "Connect Wallet" button above and select your preferred wallet provider (e.g., MetaMask, Phantom, Coinbase Wallet). Follow the prompts to securely link your wallet.
